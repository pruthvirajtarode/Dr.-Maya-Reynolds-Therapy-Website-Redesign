# Loom Walkthrough Script

**Goal:** A 5-minute client demo for Dr. Maya Reynolds.

---

**0:00–0:30 | Introduction**
"Hi Dr. Maya, this is your new website draft. Our goal was to create a digital space that feels as calm, professional, and welcoming as your actual office. We’ve moved away from generic medical designs to give you something truly premium and editorial. Let’s take a look."

**0:30–1:30 | Hero + First Impression**
"Right away, the homepage opens with a powerful first impression. We’re using a warm cream and deep forest green palette that immediately sets a grounding tone. The typography is elegant and sophisticated, and the main message focuses directly on your ideal client: finding clarity when life feels overwhelming. The 'Book a Consultation' button is clear and immediately accessible."

**1:30–2:15 | About Maya + Approach**
"As we scroll down, we transition into a space dedicated to you and your approach. We’ve kept the layout intentionally spacious. Here, we outline your background and who you work with—specifically high-achievers and professionals. We want visitors to read this and immediately say, 'She understands exactly what I'm going through.'"

**2:15–3:00 | Services**
"Next, we highlight your three core areas of focus: Anxiety & Panic, Trauma & PTSD, and Burnout & Perfectionism. Instead of basic text boxes, we’ve treated these like editorial features, pairing each with a carefully selected, calming image that visually reinforces the feeling of relief and progress."

**3:00–3:45 | Our Office**
"We also added a brand new section to showcase your Santa Monica office. Since the environment is such an important part of making clients feel safe, we created a gallery-style layout here. It highlights the warm natural light, comfortable seating, and the overall peaceful, private atmosphere they can expect when they walk through your doors."

**3:45–4:30 | FAQ + Contact Experience**
"Towards the bottom, we’ve included a clean, interactive FAQ section to answer common questions and reduce any friction before they reach out. Finally, the contact section is designed to be completely frictionless. It asks just the right questions to prepare you for the consultation, without overwhelming them."

**4:30–5:00 | Mobile Walkthrough + Final Summary**
"I also want to quickly show you how this looks on a phone. We didn't just stack things; we carefully adjusted the spacing, typography, and menu so the premium feel translates perfectly to mobile users. We're thrilled with how this has come together to elevate your practice's brand, and we'd love to hear your thoughts."
